Interfaceful is a **single bootable appliance** that connects read-only to the systems you already run, builds one continuously-reconciled picture of your infrastructure across vendors, and lets your team query it in plain language, over a REST API, or through MCP — with **all collection, storage, processing and AI inference inside your perimeter**. No telemetry, no cloud dependency, no "phone home".

### The pilot release

- **Read-only by design** — observe, reconcile and explain network state; never changes device configuration.
- **Unified inventory** across switches, routers, firewalls, controllers and scanners.
- **Change tracking** and scanner-correlated **exposure findings**.
- **A local-model operations assistant** — your data and its answers never leave the box.
- **Sovereign & air-gapped** — operates indefinitely with no Internet access.
